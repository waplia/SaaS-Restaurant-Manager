12. Family / Group Ordering QR

13. Split Cart by Person
Let each person add items under their name for easier split billing.

14. Smart Service Timer
Track time from order placed → accepted → KOT → preparing → ready → served → billed.

15. Staff Panic Button
Staff can instantly alert manager for angry customer, emergency, kitchen issue, payment issue, or equipment failure.

16. VIP Customer Alert
Notify owner/manager when high-value or repeat VIP customer visits/orders.

17. Customer Blacklist / Risk Flag
Flag abusive customers, fake orders, unpaid customers, refund abuse, and troublemaker customers.

18. Menu Item Lifecycle
Track item lifecycle: draft, testing, active, bestseller, declining, discontinued.

19. New Item Launch Tracker
Track first 7/15/30 days of new item views, orders, repeat purchase, ratings, feedback, and profitability.

20. Staff Tip Management
Track cash/online tips by staff, order, table, shift, split rules, and payroll connection.

21. Kitchen Equipment Maintenance
Track ovens, fridge, fryer, printer, AC, generator, POS devices, repair history, reminders, and maintenance cost.

22. Temperature Log
Track fridge/freezer/kitchen temperature logs for food safety and alerts.

23. Cleaning Proof Gallery
Staff uploads cleaning proof photos for kitchen, tables, washroom, storage, before/after.

24. Incident Reporting
Log incidents such as customer complaint, staff conflict, equipment failure, food issue, accident, or safety issue.

25. Daily Manager Handover
End-of-shift notes for cash issue, stock issue, staff issue, pending orders, complaints, and tomorrow tasks.

26. Owner Morning Briefing
Daily morning summary: yesterday sales, cash, low stock, staff attendance, complaints, forecast, pending tasks.

27. End-of-Day Auto Summary
Auto daily close summary with sales, expenses, profit, orders, best items, issues, and alerts.

28. Smart Closing Checklist
Before closing, check unpaid bills, open tables, pending KOT, cash mismatch, low stock, and unsynced orders.

29. Restaurant Timeline
Chronological activity feed: orders, payments, stock changes, staff actions, campaigns, complaints, refunds, and setting changes.

30. Owner Approval Inbox
One screen for all approvals: refund, discount, stock adjustment, leave, purchase order, price change, campaign, expense.

31. Customer Mood Tracking
Collect customer mood/emojis and track by day, staff, item, outlet, and order type.

32. Smart Complaint Escalation
Escalate unresolved complaints to manager/owner after configured time.

33. Repeat Complaint Detector
Detect repeated complaints about same item, staff, outlet, delivery zone, or shift.

34. Order Accuracy Score
Track wrong items, missing items, cancelled KOT, corrections, complaints, and generate accuracy score.

35. Packaging Control
Track packaging items like boxes, spoons, tissues, bags, stickers, sauce sachets, and auto-deduct by order type.

36. Sauce / Condiment Inventory
Track ketchup, chutney, dips, sauces, disposable spoons, and small packaging items.

37. Portion Control Tracker
Compare expected recipe consumption vs actual stock consumption to detect over-portioning or leakage.

38. Recipe Versioning
Track old/new recipe versions, ingredient changes, cost difference, approval history, and taste notes.

39. Taste Testing Notes
Record tasting notes, staff rating, trial customer feedback, cost, and approval before launching new item.

40. Menu Photo Approval
For chains/franchises, outlet-uploaded menu photos require brand/admin approval.

41. Brand Asset Library
Store approved logos, food photos, banners, social creatives, QR designs, packaging artwork, and brand templates.

42. Local Area Marketing Map
Map customers/orders by area, city, pincode, delivery zone, strong/weak areas.

43. Delivery Zone Profitability
Show profit by delivery zone after delivery cost, discounts, refunds, failed delivery, and delays.

44. Customer Visit Frequency Calendar
Calendar view of regular customer visits and drop-off patterns.

45. Festival Calendar for Restaurants
Campaign calendar for Diwali, Holi, Eid, Christmas, IPL, Valentine’s Day, New Year, and local festivals.

46. Offer Conflict Detector
Warn if multiple offers/coupons overlap and reduce margin too much.

47. Margin Guardrails
Warn before discount/campaign if margin falls below configured threshold.

48. Smart Upsell Script for Waiters
Show waiter-friendly upsell lines and track acceptance rate.

49. Staff Leaderboard TV Mode
Display staff leaderboard for sales, upsells, reviews, fastest service, and attendance.

50. Customer-Facing Order Status Screen
Customer can see live order status: accepted, preparing, ready, served, delivered.
