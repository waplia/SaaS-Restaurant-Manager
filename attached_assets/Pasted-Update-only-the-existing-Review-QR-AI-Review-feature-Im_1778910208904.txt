Goal:
The Review QR should work automatically for restaurant customers after scanning QR.

Customer flow:
1. Customer scans restaurant Review QR.
2. Open existing public review page.
3. Customer selects rating from 1 to 5 stars.
4. Customer selects quick feedback tags.
5. Customer can optionally write feedback.
6. AI generates a Google review draft based on customer rating, selected tags, and feedback.
7. Show generated review text to customer in editable text box.
8. Customer can edit the review.
9. Customer can copy the review.
10. Customer clicks “Open Google Review”.
11. Redirect customer to the restaurant’s saved Google review link.
12. Customer manually pastes and submits review on Google.

Very important:
- No Google API required.
- No auto-posting to Google.
- No Google login integration.
- No Google review fetching.
- No Google reply posting.
- Only use the restaurant’s Google review URL saved in settings.
- Keep it simple and working.
