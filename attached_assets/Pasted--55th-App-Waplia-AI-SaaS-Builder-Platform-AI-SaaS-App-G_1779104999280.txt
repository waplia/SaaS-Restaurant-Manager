# 55th App: Waplia AI SaaS Builder Platform

## AI SaaS App Generator + Multi-Tenant SaaS OS + Modules + Auth + Billing + Admin + Deployment Builder

This app is for:

* SaaS founders
* agencies building SaaS for clients
* internal product teams
* no-code/low-code SaaS builders
* software resellers
* white-label SaaS businesses
* startup studios
* Waplia internal app generation
* client portal builders
* business software builders

It should let users plan, create, configure, and manage SaaS products with:

**SaaS idea → app structure → modules → database schema → roles → pricing → onboarding → dashboard → billing integration → tenant setup → launch checklist → analytics → AI product manager**
