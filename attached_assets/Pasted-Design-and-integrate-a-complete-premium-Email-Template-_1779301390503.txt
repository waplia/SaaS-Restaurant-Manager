Design and integrate a complete premium Email Template System in Super Admin for KhanaLagao.

Current requirement:
Super Admin should have attractive, modern, editable email templates for every important platform activity, and those templates should be integrated everywhere in the platform, like signup, OTP, login, plan upgrade, trial, payment, invoice, support, AI credits, restaurant onboarding, follow-ups, and customer activity emails.

Important:
- Do not create basic plain emails.
- Design premium, modern, responsive HTML email templates.
- All templates should be editable from Super Admin.
- All templates should support variables/placeholders.
- All email sending should use Super Admin configured email provider only.
- Restaurant admin should not manage SMTP/API keys.
- Do not break existing email provider/settings.
- No mock success.
- Every sent email must be logged.
- Every template should have preview and test email option.
- Every activity should use the matching template automatically.
- If provider is not configured, show clear error and do not fail silently.

Brand:
KhanaLagao

Theme:
Modern restaurant-tech SaaS
Orange, warm white, dark navy
Premium cards
Clear CTA button
Mobile responsive
Clean footer
Professional layout
