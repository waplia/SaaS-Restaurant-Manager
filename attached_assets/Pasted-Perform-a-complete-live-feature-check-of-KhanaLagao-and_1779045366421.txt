Every important basic feature/add-on should work from start to end across Public Website, Super Admin, Restaurant Admin, POS, QR Menu, Kitchen, Inventory, Staff, Payroll, Customers, Growth, Khana AI, Finance, Reports, Mobile App, Notifications, and Settings.
