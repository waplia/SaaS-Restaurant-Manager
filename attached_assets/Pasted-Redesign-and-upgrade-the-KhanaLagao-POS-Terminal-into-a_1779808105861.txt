Redesign and upgrade the KhanaLagao POS Terminal into a modern full-page restaurant POS.

Current requirement:
POS should open in a separate full-page / full-screen route, not inside a small dashboard section or cramped layout. It should feel like a real modern restaurant POS with fast billing, item search, category browsing, cart, table/order modes, calculator, sound/tone feedback, keyboard shortcuts, printing, payments, and all important restaurant POS features.

Important:
- Do not break existing orders, menu, tables, KOT, kitchen, payments, reports, inventory, or QR order flow.
- Do not use mock data.
- POS must use real menu items, real prices, real add-ons, real inventory, real tables, and real customers.
- Every button must work or show a clear permission/configuration message.
- POS should be fast, touch-friendly, tablet-friendly, and desktop-friendly.
- No infinite loading.
- No raw ID fields.
- All selections must be dropdown/search/real data.

Route:
Open POS in a dedicated full-page route:

/pos
/restaurant/pos
or existing route, but full-screen.

Add button:
Dashboard → Open POS

When clicked:
Open POS in full-page mode.
Optional:
Open in new tab button: “Open Full POS”
