17. UPI QR PRINTING WITH PRINTER
==================================================

When restaurant profile has UPI QR enabled:

- Generate UPI QR from restaurant/outlet UPI ID.
- Print QR on customer bill only.
- QR should include final bill amount.
- QR should use invoice/order reference.
- QR should fit correctly on 58mm and 80mm paper.
- QR should be readable after printing.
- Add QR print preview.
- Add test UPI QR print.

UPI QR format:

upi://pay?pa={upi_id}&pn={merchant_name}&am={bill_amount}&cu=INR&tn={bill_or_order_note}&tr={order_or_invoice_number}

Fallback:
- If UPI QR enabled but UPI ID missing, show admin warning.
- Do not crash print flow.
- Print bill without QR if UPI ID is missing and log warning.
