====================================================
FINAL PLATFORM FLOW
====================================================

The complete platform should work like this:

1. Public Website
- Visitor opens KhanaLagao website.
- Website looks premium, modern, fast, mobile responsive, and SEO friendly.
- Visitor can understand all features clearly.
- Visitor can view pricing, features, Khana AI, Growth Engine, solutions, blog, about, contact.
- Visitor can submit Book Demo / Contact / Partner form.
- Lead should be saved in Super Admin.
- Visitor can click Login or Start Free Trial.

2. Super Admin Flow
- Super Admin logs in from separate secure login.
- Super Admin can manage:
  - Restaurants
  - Plans
  - Subscriptions
  - Payments
  - Invoices
  - Leads
  - Website CMS
  - Feature flags
  - AI providers
  - AI credits
  - SMS settings
  - WhatsApp settings
  - Email settings
  - Payment gateways
  - Marketplace
  - Add-ons
  - Reports
  - Support tickets
  - Audit logs
  - System settings
- Super Admin can create restaurant account manually.
- Super Admin can assign plan and modules.
- Super Admin can enable/disable features per plan or restaurant.
- Super Admin can add AI credits.
- Super Admin can view SaaS revenue, restaurant usage, and investor-style metrics.

3. Restaurant Onboarding Flow
- Restaurant owner signs up or is created by Super Admin.
- Restaurant owner logs in.
- Setup wizard should guide owner:
  - Restaurant profile
  - Outlet setup
  - Business hours
  - Tax/GST settings
  - Currency/timezone
  - Table/floor setup
  - Menu setup or AI menu import
  - Payment settings
  - QR menu settings
  - Staff setup
  - Printer/KOT settings
- After onboarding, owner reaches restaurant dashboard.

4. Restaurant Dashboard Flow
- Dashboard shows:
  - Today sales
  - Live orders
  - Kitchen status
  - Tables status
  - Inventory alerts
  - Staff attendance
  - Customer activity
  - Revenue chart
  - AI insights
  - Pending actions
- Dashboard should not show fake data unless demo mode is enabled.
- If no data, show helpful empty states.

5. Daily Restaurant Operation Flow
- Cashier opens POS Terminal.
- Cashier creates dine-in/takeaway/delivery order.
- Order creates bill and KOT.
- Kitchen receives KOT/KDS.
- Table status updates.
- Inventory deducts stock based on recipe.
- Payment is collected.
- Invoice is generated.
- Customer is saved in CRM.
- Reports update automatically.

6. QR Menu Flow
- Owner creates QR menu.
- Customer scans table QR.
- Customer browses menu.
- Customer adds items.
- Customer places order.
- Order appears in restaurant dashboard/POS/Kitchen.
- Staff accepts/prepares/serves order.
- Payment can be collected depending on settings.

7. Review QR Flow
- Customer scans review QR.
- Customer selects rating.
- Customer selects feedback tags.
- Customer optionally writes feedback.
- AI generates review draft if enabled and credits are available.
- Customer can edit/copy review.
- Customer clicks Open Google Review.
- Customer manually pastes and submits on Google.
- No Google API is required.
- Feedback and analytics are saved.

8. Khana AI Flow
- Restaurant uses Khana AI features.
- AI credits are checked before request.
- AI output is generated.
- Credits are deducted only after successful generation.
- Usage logs are saved.
- If credits are not enough, show recharge/upgrade message.
- Super Admin can control provider, model, credits, and limits.

9. Growth Engine Flow
- Owner creates campaign.
- Selects customer segment.
- Selects channel: WhatsApp/SMS/email/in-app.
- Creates or AI-generates content.
- Reviews and approves campaign.
- Campaign is scheduled or sent.
- Logs and analytics are saved.

10. Finance Flow
- Payments, expenses, wallet, settlements, invoices, and P&L are tracked.
- Owner can see real profit, not only sales.
- Reports can be filtered/exported.

11. Support Flow
- Restaurant can raise ticket.
- Super Admin/support team can reply.
- Ticket status updates.
- All communication is logged.
