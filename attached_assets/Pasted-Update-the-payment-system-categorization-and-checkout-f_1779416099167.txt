==================================================
4. ONLINE PAYMENT OWNERSHIP LOGIC
==================================================

If restaurant selects Platform Gateway:
- Payment goes to platform gateway account.
- Webhook verifies payment.
- Platform records gateway fee, platform fee, settlement amount.
- Restaurant receives settlement based on settlement rules.

If restaurant selects Own Gateway:
- Payment goes directly to restaurant gateway account.
- Restaurant credentials are stored encrypted.
- Webhook still updates order status in platform.
- Super Admin can see transaction metadata but cannot see secret keys.

If restaurant selects Own Manual UPI:
- Restaurant adds UPI ID/VPA and merchant name.
- System generates UPI intent link and QR dynamically.
- Customer can open PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, BHIM, etc.
- Customer can copy UPI ID.
- Customer can enter UTR/reference number.
- Customer can upload screenshot optional.
- Order status becomes manual_verification_pending.
- Restaurant/admin must verify and mark payment as manually_verified.
- Do not auto-mark manual UPI as paid only from app callback.

Manual UPI is online customer self-payment, but it is not gateway-verified. It requires manual verification unless bank statement reconciliation is added later.

upi://pay?pa={upi_id}&pn={merchant_name}&am={amount}&cu=INR&tn={order_note}&tr={order_reference}

Customer flow:
1. Customer chooses Pay Online.
2. Customer selects Manual UPI / UPI QR.
3. App shows QR and button "Pay with UPI App".
4. Customer pays using any UPI app.
5. Customer enters UTR/reference number.
6. Customer uploads screenshot optional.
7. Order moves to manual_verification_pending.
8. Restaurant verifies payment.
9. Order becomes manually_verified/paid.

Do not mark manual UPI as paid only because user clicked "I have paid".
