# Info.plist permission strings

Capacitor will generate `ios/App/App/Info.plist` after `cap add ios`.

Even though we don't currently call any sensitive iOS APIs from the WebView,
Apple App Review will reject the binary if you later add a feature (camera,
photo library, location, etc.) without a usage description. Add these keys
the moment a feature is enabled:

- `NSCameraUsageDescription` — "Khana Lagao uses your camera to scan QR menus
  and upload dish photos."
- `NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription` — "Khana Lagao uses your photo library so
  you can attach dish images to your menu."
- `NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription` — "Khana Lagao uses your location to
  show nearby branches."
- `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription` — only if voice notes are added later.

Also confirm:

- `CFBundleDisplayName` = `Khana Lagao`
- `CFBundleIdentifier` = `com.khanalagao.app`
- `UIApplicationSceneManifest` is present (generated by Capacitor).
- `NSAppTransportSecurity` is left at defaults (all requests over HTTPS).
